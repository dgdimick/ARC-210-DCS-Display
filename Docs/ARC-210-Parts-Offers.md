# ARC-210 PCB and printed-parts offers

**Last updated: October 31, 2026 at 1:52 AM MDT (America/Denver)**

I offer these parts to help other builders complete my ARC-210 panel. **These offers are for the ARC-210 only. I don’t currently offer PCB or printed-parts sets for the ARC-164 or ARC-186.** All prices below are in **US dollars (USD)**. This is the page I’ll update when my prices, availability, materials, or shipping terms change.

## Bare PCB set

**Price: US$40, plus shipping and handling.**

The complete set contains **six bare boards** for one ARC-210 panel:

- One Main Board v1.1
- One Button Matrix PCB v3.2
- One Rotary Encoder PCB v1.4
- Three Rotary Switch PCBs v2.0, including the optional SQL On/Off board

This is a convenient way to get the boards for one radio without managing four design uploads or ordering extra copies of each design. You’ll supply the electronics, faceplate, printed parts, and assembly. Assembled-board electrical testing is still pending. The current Button Matrix PCB does not support LED backlighting; see [Future Thoughts](Future-Thoughts.md) for the possible redesign.

My own order for all the boards cost about US$75. That was my approximate whole-order cost, not the manufacturing cost of one six-board set or a quote for another builder. Whether my set saves you money depends on the full delivered cost for your location; I’m offering convenience rather than a guaranteed saving. You can also [order the boards directly from JLCPCB](PCB-Manufacturing-JLCPCB.md).

## Printed parts

**Price: US$3 (three US dollars), plus shipping.**

If you don’t have access to a 3D printer and that is stopping you from building my ARC-210 panel, I’ll send you **one complete 17-part printed set**. I’ll print the **mounting bracket in black PETG** and the **remaining parts in gray PETG**. I want the lack of a printer to be one less obstacle to building your SIMPIT.

The set contains nine knobs, one display mounting bracket, six encoder spacers, and one SQL shaft spacer, matching the [current master print set](../Supporting%20STL%27s/README.md#complete-17-part-print-set). These are printed parts only; boards, electronics, and the faceplate are not included. If you can print your own parts, the [STL and 3MF downloads](../Supporting%20STL%27s/README.md) remain available.

## Shipping, VAT, and import charges

Shipping is separate for both offers, and handling is also separate for the PCB set. Contact me for availability and a shipping quote before arranging a set.

I’ve already paid tax on my board order, and I’m offering these sets as part of my personal SIMPIT project rather than a commercial venture. I don’t plan to add a separate sales-tax charge to my PCB-set price. **I’ll only collect VAT if I’m required to. If I’m not required to collect it, I won’t.**

Even if I’m not required to collect VAT, you may still have to pay **import VAT, customs duties, or carrier clearance fees** in your country. I don’t control those charges, and they aren’t included in my prices.
