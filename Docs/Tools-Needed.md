# Tools needed

**Last updated: November 12, 2026 at 9:32 PM MST (America/Denver)**

- Hot glue gun, or an electronics-grade, neutral-cure silicone adhesive such as **MG Chemicals 1035**, for securing wires and the TFT display
- Soldering iron
- 3D printer, or access to one
- Flush wire cutters
- Safety glasses
- Magnifying device (optional). I use one to inspect solder joints.
- Bright work light so you can see the pads and leads clearly while soldering.
- PCB holder or bench vise. Holding the PCB steady makes soldering much easier. I use a PanaVise, and any good-quality bench vise that can hold the board securely will do. [PanaVise on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=panavise)
- USB Micro-B cable for programming the Teensy 4.1 and connecting the unit to the computer
